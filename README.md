## About me

I'm a mobile developer based in Gdansk, Poland, working with **Android, Kotlin Multiplatform, and Compose Multiplatform**. I also develop web applications and backend services in **TypeScript and Kotlin**, taking complete features from initial design through implementation.

At **Benched**, I work across the frontend and backend on checkout, payments, and the order lifecycle. I'm also pursuing a **Master's in Computer Science at the University of Gdansk**.

I enjoy the details that make an app feel good to use: clear interfaces, adaptive layouts, and smooth animations. Outside development, I sing and play the piano and guitar. 🎶

## Selected work

### Benched · Fitness goods marketplace

Full-stack development of checkout and orders, including validation, refunds, and referral logic. Implemented a Stripe Connect payment flow that holds funds on the platform until buyer approval, and integrated InPost ShipX for delivery and tracking.

### Ticks · Interval training app

Independently developed an Android and iOS app with Compose Multiplatform, sharing nearly all application code. Implemented a custom interval-training engine and an animated UI with navigation that collapses on scroll and a button that morphs into a search field.

### ERASMUS Adventure · Visual novel

Implemented character entrances and positioning, story checkpoints, and animations. Built layouts for portrait, landscape, and foldable screens, collaborated with a writer and designer, and introduced a junior developer to Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

### Love Calendar · Full-stack Android app

An app for long-distance couples to plan meetings and track important dates. Built with Jetpack Compose and MVI, backed by a unit-tested Ktor REST API with JWT authentication and MongoDB.

[Android app](https://github.com/pavel-maiseichyk/love-calendar) · [Backend](https://github.com/pavel-maiseichyk/love-calendar-api)

### Curriculum Management Platform · University of Split

Transformed a 100k+ line JSON file into structured timetable data and independently designed and implemented a Next.js frontend, working with the supervising professor throughout development.

### EcoGarden · Waste-sorting gamification app

Developed the Android client and Ktor backend as the sole developer. Implemented Google Sign-In, a reward system, and an animated virtual garden, with sorting activity stored using Room.
